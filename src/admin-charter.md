# Admin Charter

## Purpose

The original governance structure was way too complicated. Let's simplify it!

That's the only TL;DR you get. The rest is just good context.

The original situation we had was, um...
* The Admins
* 5 "Admin teams" (not confusing at all)
    * Inconsistent purposes; some driving the server, some driving the projects, leading to inconsistent rule structures

Plus a prototype corporate structure bolted on top, leading to too many meetings, overly-strict interpretations of Governance, and a ton of misunderstandings compounded with a lack of general empathy. Which sucked. So we're not going to do that again.

The new structure is much simpler:
* the LC Admins, reabsorbing about half of the Admin Teams
* the LC Corporate Leadership, absorbing all the parts of one certain team it should've been handling in the first place (for clarity, that would be the LC Management Team)
* the LCHQ Staff, reorienting to handle the community side of the Discord. Only, and entirely.

There are caveats and subdivisions within this new structure, but overall the responsibilities are finally where they're supposed to be. The Discord server runs like a Discord server, the company runs like a company, and the Admins pull the strings like, uh, a happy little shadow government. But benevolent.

## LC Admins

The LC Admins (hereafter Admins) will resume authority over:
* Members,
* LC Projects,
* Establishment of new ILM Projects,
* Oversight and Adjudication in case of controversy of the other parts of the LC Administration

The initial membership of the Admins shall be:
* Connor
* Emmy
* Matthew
* Ray

New Admins may be added by unanimous consent of the Admins. Admins may be removed by unanimous consent of the other Admins. Though this rule, like all other Admin rules, is flexible and open to change if necessary.

The Admins should generally gain majority approval before taking actions. A 24-hour Final Comment Period starts at majority approval and is automatically waived at unanimous approval. Where it is reasonable to do so for the given decision, an Admins decision should be made publically, where all Members can review the decision. A decision being made publically does not necessarily expand the list of people entitled to approve it, nor does it necessarily mandate a final comment period.

The vision of LC may be unilaterally set by any member of the Admins. Customarily, one member is appointed as the Vision Caster of LC; this person is tasked with declaring broad statements about the purpose of LC and its constituent groups. This person is encouraged, but not required, to seek consensus among the Admins before making public declarations. Currently, the Vision Caster of LC is Ray.

## LC Corporate Leadership

The LC Corporate Leadership is split into two parts:
* The LC Corporate Administration, and
* The LC Corporate Executive.

The LC Corporate Administration will take over the projects previously owned by the LC Management Team (CtC and OPL).

The LC Corporate Executive will be composed of the Officers of the LC Corporation and will be defined by the By-Laws of the Corporation. There will be a Liason Officer between the LC Corporate Administration and the LC Corporate Executive. 

The Current Membership of the LC Management Team will become the initial Membership of the LC Corporate Administration, and new members of the LC Corporate Administration may be appointed by the Admins with the consent of the Team. The LC Corporate Executive will be appointed by the Board of Directors.

The LC Corporate Administration will make decisions by consensus.

## LCHQ Staff

The LCHQ Staff is responsible for maintaining and moderating the Discord server.

New Staff members may be added by general consensus of the Staff and may be similarly removed.

Staff members may opt to volunteer as a Moderator. This is simply handled by a role that a Staff member can give or remove from themself. It carries the moderation permissions as well as a pingable role (@Moderator), used by the community to flag down issues.

As the server is currently split into two effective segments (with the project side holding some confidential information), the Admins jointly hold the server ownership account. We hope this isn't an issue in the short-term. Once the projects move out, the server owner role may be given directly to a consistently-active Staff member, or the account may still be jointly-owned but now controlled by the Staff.

If the server changes names, the name of this team changes too. For example, if the server name were to change to "LC Landing", the team would be the "LC Landing Staff".

## Public Decision Making

Both the LC Admins and the LC Corporate Administration should make decisions in public unless there is not likely to be a Member interest in review of the decision or making the decision in private is necessary to preserve the privacy interests of the Members of LC or of LC as a whole.

The LC Corporate Executive shall be subject to oversight in a manner that permits the LC Corporate Administration and the Members as a whole to review the conduct to the greatest extent reasonable. 

For the purposes of these two teams, "in public" means in a manner reasonably accessible to any Member of LC.
